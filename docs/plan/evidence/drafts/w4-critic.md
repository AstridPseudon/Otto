## 1. Over-engineering: cuts and merges

1. **Replace the eleven entity types in `w3-structure.md` §2 with a queue, task results, and linked artifacts.** Keep one program/project brief, one authoritative project queue, and a result record per task or attempt. Put decisions and handoff notes in the brief’s history; link test logs and evidence from task results. Generate status and digests from the queue. This preserves durable evidence and handoffs while avoiding separate `Review`, `Escalation`, `Check-in`, `Handoff`, `Evidence`, and `Cost` systems. The queue and `STATUS.md` already duplicated some task state, and run results were spread across files and markers. [out/w2-tracking.md, findings 1–3, 7; out/w2-supervision.md, finding 7]

2. **Remove the manual check-in JSON and most repeated status fields.** Keep current state, blocker or wait reason, last update, and next action on the queue/run; derive queue counts, active workers, and “last landing” from it. The structure draft’s separate check-in repeats those facts, while snapshot lag was a separate problem: snapshots concealed landings even with hourly oversight. [w3-structure.md §§2–3; out/w2-tracking.md, finding 4; out/w2-supervision.md, findings 1, 8]

3. **Make “reviewer” an optional assignment mode, not a permanent role and entity.** A review can be a read-only task linked to the revision and its findings. Keep a distinct reviewer only when the work needs independent judgment. Per-task reviews were later removed from the manager process, a deep check was skipped as duplicative, and TST’s proposed review cadence differed from UPD’s. [out/w2-codified-process.md, finding 3; out/w2-reviews.md; 10-28 05:51–06:58]

4. **Delete `difficulty_score`, its four-factor rubric, and the universal tier mapping from `w3-structure.md` §5.** Record the actual model/backend on a run; retain an abstract tier only when the owner has set one for that phase. The tier-to-model mapping changed with provider availability, and the records do not compare quality or cost by tier. The proposed score and cutoffs are explicitly inferred, not calibrated. [out/w2-models-cost.md, findings 1, 5–6; 10-27 20:54–20:55; 10-28 02:07–02:08, 10:31]

5. **Drop the fixed 5-minute, 15-minute, and 100K-token triggers.** Keep event-based signals: a dispatched worker has no start, ready work sits idle, a completed result awaits review, or a snapshot predates a landing. Show timestamps and let the project set any numeric thresholds. The incidents justify visibility, but none of those proposed numeric limits was tested. The hourly loop also failed operationally and was later turned off at the owner’s request. [w3-structure.md §4; out/w2-throughput.md, findings 1, 7–8; 10-27 13:02–16:06; 10-28 18:09–18:24]

6. **Make acceptance approval conditional, not an extra universal round trip.** Require a clear deliverable and proof for implementation tasks; ask for a proposal-and-approval step only when acceptance is missing or ambiguous. For exploration, define the question and required evidence instead of requiring a product test. The proposal gate was introduced after a cold-start review found missing criteria; the worker-brief lens treats it as a response to missing acceptance, and explicitly allows learning outcomes for exploratory tasks. [w3-structure.md §6; w3-principles.md “Planning”; out/w2-verification.md, finding 1; out/w2-worker-briefs.md, candidate principles]

7. **Cut the platform’s first version to queue, dispatch, results, and handoff.** Defer per-task cost attribution, automated cadence controls, a full authorization registry, and a separate test-run entity until the file workflow shows they are needed. Preserve exact base/revision, evidence links, not-run status, and a portable handoff manifest. The records support these concrete needs; they do not show that a full normalized data model or every proposed automated control is required. [w3-structure.md §§2, 7; out/w2-tracking.md, findings 4–6; out/w2-models-cost.md, finding 6]

## 2. Evidence check: revise or qualify claims

1. **Soften causal claims about throughput.** The drafts say back-to-back dispatch and parallelism “improved throughput” and that fresh managers “helped” phase boundaries. The observed sequence is that the owner corrected self-scheduled idling, then eight named tasks landed from 00:32–01:01; fresh managers were used at phase/provider transitions. That shows timing and use, not a measured causal effect. Change to “followed the dispatch correction” and “were used to mark phase boundaries.” [w3-retro.md, “What worked” items 6 and 9; out/w2-throughput.md, findings 2 and 5; 10-28 00:32–01:01]

2. **Qualify the success of acceptance gates.** The cold-start review found missing criteria and the process added a proposal/approval step; the records show the rule was used, but not a before/after reliability comparison. Say “made the expected proof explicit in these cases,” not that it generally made completion reliable. [w3-retro.md, scorecard 10-26 19:59–20:06 and “What worked” item 2; out/w2-verification.md, finding 1]

3. **Treat model-tier policy as owner preference, not demonstrated effectiveness.** The owner specified Luna/Terra/Sol, and Opus’s role was corrected, but the model mapping changed across providers and the cost log does not compare cost or outcomes by task or tier. Lower confidence in `w3-principles.md`’s tiering principle and remove the structure draft’s difficulty rubric. [out/w2-models-cost.md, findings 1, 5–6; 10-28 01:29–01:40, 10:31]

4. **Do not claim hourly supervision was a proven default.** Hourly oversight was requested and documented; on Oct 27 its loop was missing, the owner found no work running, and later the owner turned hourly review off. Keep the operational lesson—checks must reveal liveness and stale state—but describe cadence as owner-set and adjustable. [w3-principles.md, “Supervision”; w3-structure.md §4; out/w2-supervision.md, findings 1–2; 10-27 13:02–16:06; 10-28 18:09–18:24]

5. **Distinguish correlation from cause in the cost discussion.** Context reached 263K–281K tokens while costs rose, and the lens calls repeated context reads a likely cost driver. The evidence does not isolate context size as the cause of the spend. Revise “large context drove material model spend” to “large context coincided with high spend; repeated reads were identified as a likely contributor.” Per-task cost fields cannot be justified as a proven solution by this run’s session-level cost log. [w3-retro.md, “What went badly” item 7; out/w2-antipatterns.md, finding 6; out/w2-models-cost.md, finding 6; 10-28 01:57–03:57]

6. **Keep the failure-count caveat wherever those counts appear.** The reported 82 + 10 + 5 totals 97 against 87 IDs; the sources do not reconcile overlap or counting. Retain only the supported finding that changing the baseline to `origin/main` surfaced regression candidates, and do not present the counts as a complete partition. The retro and principles drafts note this; the structure draft’s result-class discussion should carry the same caveat. [w3-principles.md, “Source conflicts and limits”; out/w2-antipatterns.md, finding 4; 10-28 13:04–14:17]

7. **Clarify “UPD completed.”** The queue closed, but the later rebase had 1,021 tests unrun and Windows, macOS, CI, and live GitHub checks were “written, not run.” Say “UPD implementation queue closed” wherever “completed” could imply full verification or release. The retro already names the verification gap; make its phase summary consistent with that distinction. [w3-retro.md, “Program in one page” and “End state”; `REBASE-COMPLETE.md`; 10-28 20:38–20:56]

## 3. Gaps: lessons from the lens reports missing or too weak

1. **Document drift needs a direct lesson, not just a file-cleanup note.** The operating model changed, but README and other instructions retained older roles or CI assumptions; the TST plan and queue also disagreed on task/review counts. Add: maintain one current read-first brief and reconcile it when a standing rule or plan changes; mark old instructions superseded. [out/w2-codified-process.md, findings 1, 7; out/w2-setup-explore.md, finding 7]

2. **Represent the requested work mode explicitly.** The owner distinguished investigation from implementation, planning from queueing, and updating a zip from executing the next phase. Add a small `mode` or plain-language scope field such as investigate, plan, implement, or package-only; carry it into the next action. [out/w2-owner-signals.md, finding 7; 10-27 21:50; 10-28 16:24–16:55]

3. **Separate owner absence from runtime health.** “No questions” authorized in-scope judgment overnight; it did not mean the manager or workers were alive. The drafts cover authority and liveness, but do not make this distinction explicit. Add one brief rule: record what decisions may be made while the owner is away, and separately report whether the execution loop is alive. [out/w2-autonomy.md, findings 1, 6–7; 10-28 02:08; 10-27 14:56–16:06]

4. **Add a minimal unattended-spend guard, if unattended work is part of the design.** The autonomy lens notes overnight spend of about $59 and no evident hard spending limit; the structure draft proposes detailed cost fields but no ceiling. Prefer one optional phase/session budget with a notification or stop rule over task-by-task cost accounting. [out/w2-autonomy.md, candidate principles; out/w2-models-cost.md, finding 6; `COST-LOG.md`; 10-28 02:57–09:57]

5. **Make dispatch/tool preflight a concrete step.** Four workers failed their first command before this was detected, and later remote Codex and hosted CI availability differed from assumptions. Add a one-worker start check and confirm the intended runner/destination before fan-out. The drafts mention preflight in principles, but do not make this the simple activation check in the platform structure. [out/w2-setup-explore.md, findings 2, 5; out/w2-throughput.md, finding 1; 10-26 22:57–23:38; 10-28 10:31–10:44, 15:50–16:13]

## 4. Contradictions between drafts

1. **Review policy:** `w3-principles.md` asks for separate robustness and simplicity reviews and says to close with “two reviews”; `w3-structure.md` defines a reviewer role/entity; the retro and source-limit section say review intensity varied and three rounds are not a default. Replace these with one optional, focused review that checks both missing safeguards and removable machinery; add another only for a distinct unresolved risk. [w3-principles.md, principles 4 and 10, “Second opinions”; w3-structure.md §§1–2, 6; w3-retro.md, “Source conflicts and limits”; out/w2-reviews.md]

2. **Test-result vocabulary:** `w3-structure.md` lists five result classes; `w3-principles.md` adds `release-pending`; `out/w2-verification.md` also distinguishes skipped and hung/not-run, while new tests absent on the base cannot be directly classified as pre-existing. Separate execution status (`passed`, `failed`, `not run`) from failure explanation (for example, regression, pre-existing, environmental, release-pending, or no baseline). [w3-structure.md §2; w3-principles.md principle 8; out/w2-verification.md, findings 3–5; 10-28 15:27–15:29, 20:56]

3. **Cadence:** `w3-principles.md` and `w3-structure.md` prescribe hourly checks during active work, but the owner eventually turned hourly review off, and the prior loop failed to run. State that cadence is an owner/project setting; the queue should continue independently of check-ins. [w3-principles.md, principle 7 and “Supervision”; w3-structure.md §4; out/w2-owner-signals.md, finding 2; 10-27 13:02–16:06; 10-28 18:09–18:24]

4. **Model routing:** `w3-principles.md` says model tiers are local policy; `w3-structure.md` then prescribes a numeric score and universal cutoffs. Remove the scoring table and keep any owner-selected tier mapping as phase-specific configuration. [w3-principles.md, principle 5 and “Task breakdown and difficulty/model assignment”; w3-structure.md §5; out/w2-models-cost.md, findings 1, 5]

5. **Task completion gates:** `w3-structure.md` requires an approved measurable `Done when` before implementation; `w3-principles.md` allows exploratory tasks to have learning/report outcomes instead. Align the gate around an explicit deliverable and proof appropriate to the task type, with a proposal step only when acceptance is unclear. [w3-structure.md §§3, 6; w3-principles.md “Planning”; out/w2-worker-briefs.md, candidate principles]

6. **Duplicate status ownership:** `w3-structure.md` calls the queue the source of truth, yet also gives Program, Project, Task, Run, and Check-in separate status fields and a check-in queue snapshot. Derive program/project summaries from the queue and keep only one editable task state. [w3-structure.md §§2–4; out/w2-tracking.md, findings 1–3]
