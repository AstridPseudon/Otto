Two facts change how several findings read:

- **REWORK landed only its Herzchen half.** c365152 added `run_continuation` (`src/herzchen/fleet/hosts/contract.py:369-389`) and its tests, but nothing in the product calls it. The Claude adapter still refuses to resume a worker (`src/otto/agents/claude_adapter.py:389`). REALWORKER 3 is therefore a REWORK follow-up, not a duplicate. ## 3. Decisions for the orchestrator

1. **The worker turn budget.** `ROLE_PROFILES['worker']['max_turns']` is 8 (`claude_adapter.py:36`). A real Haiku worker that calls `task`, reads, edits, runs `check` (perhaps twice) and calls `done` can run past 8, and `error_max_turns` settles the turn `failed`, which stalls its project today (FAILEDFENCE). *Recommendation:* raise it to about 30 inside FAILEDFENCE, record the turns each worker used in REALSTAGE3's report, and keep FAILEDFENCE's recovery either way.
2. **When dead code goes, and what stays.** DEADLAUNCH deletes the uncalled gateway, orchestrator-route and owner-runtime entries, `assign_and_start`, `PortfolioLifecycleIntegration` and `ReleaseOperations`, which retires FAILOPEN 2, 3 and 5 rather than fixing them. LAUNCHPATHS 5 also proposed deleting `run_turn` and `run_continuation`. *Recommendation:* land DEADLAUNCH after the stage-3 blockers rather than waiting for DELETE (no stage depends on it, but dead launch routes mislead workers that read the code). Keep `run_turn` and `run_continuation` for REWORKWIRE. Delete `ReleaseOperations` rather than fix it, unless a release feature is planned.
3. **Reversing a HOLD-era test.** PINALL reverses `test_reused_pid_after_group_death_stays_held` (`tests/test_capacity_holds.py:121`), which HOLD's "don't trust a reused PID" rule produced. *Recommendation:* reverse it. An empty recorded PGID is the proof that the group is gone. The leader's birth matters only while the group has members, and PINALL keeps the live-member test (:129).
4. **How far the "no product method patched" rule reaches.** TESTHONESTY 3 and 4 apply it to unit tests; the rule as written governs stage harnesses. *Recommendation:* apply it to tests whose names claim an owner path. Rebuild the two with a false-green scenario (hydration, ask authorization). For the other three, either rebuild them or rename them as wiring tests with one real-owner companion test each. Leave scoped failure injection alone.
5. **Owner-side retention: a new row or part of RET?** *Recommendation:* make RETOWNER's bullets part of RET (one retention design after REVIEW, from the perch's growth figures) instead of a separate row. Keep RETOWNER's invariant: never reclaim a replay fence.
