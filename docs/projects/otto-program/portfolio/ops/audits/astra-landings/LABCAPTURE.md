1. **An earlier turn’s capture can authorize deleting a later turn’s lab.** Startup matches only task ID; its predicate ignores the marker commit ([product_service.py:416](${TRANSFER_ROOT}/source/otto/src/otto/hostctl/product_service.py:416)). Markers contain only commit and branch ([labs.py:375](${TRANSFER_ROOT}/source/otto/src/herzchen/fleet/labs.py:375)). A fresh worker request is allowed after a successful prior turn ([manager_journey_service.py:2313](${TRANSFER_ROOT}/source/otto/src/otto/portfolio/manager_journey_service.py:2313)). Thus: turn A captures without landing; turn B lands, then crashes before capture; startup uses A’s Due to delete B’s lab.
   **Fix — xhard:** Bind the landing marker to its exact owner occurrence/assignment generation and require that occurrence’s capture. Change landing, labs, and registry settlement. **Failing test:** retain A’s captured Due and B’s uncaptured landing; startup must preserve B’s lab, then remove it after B’s capture.

2. **Cleanup checks identity outside the deletion lock.** `sweep_landed` checks HEAD before calling `remove` ([labs.py:423](${TRANSFER_ROOT}/source/otto/src/herzchen/fleet/labs.py:423)); `remove` acquires its lock later and unlinks the current clean lab without checking the expected marker commit ([labs.py:343](${TRANSFER_ROOT}/source/otto/src/herzchen/fleet/labs.py:343)). A writer can commit and release its lock between those operations, causing uncaptured replacement work to lose its lab. The absent-directory ref/marker cleanup also runs unlocked at line 411.
   **Fix — xhard:** Hold one exclusive lab lock across marker, capture, HEAD/ref validation, and deletion, including partial-removal recovery. **Failing test:** use barriers to change HEAD between validation and removal; cleanup must retain the replacement lab.

   **Fix — normal:** Validate capture through the selected verifier’s evidence and canonical publication, without imposing Claude’s artifact shape or equating distinct refs. **Failing test:** feed a valid captured Codex command/final result through the real registry callback and startup sweep; both must remove its eligible landed lab.
